Privacy notice

How Community Connection handles what you share.

This notice summarises how the website behaves today. It should be reviewed by the campaign’s legal adviser against the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, before launch.

What Community Connection collects

You choose what to share. Depending on your answers, a response may include whether you know or have met Dawson, your ward and the area, whether you would like a meeting and in what setting, a topic you would like to discuss, and, only if you agree to be contacted, your name, phone number and email address.

How it is used

  • To understand what matters in each ward.
  • To contact you about your request, only if you gave consent.

Who can see it

Responses are stored in a secured database. They can be read only by authorised team members who sign in to the administrator portal. Details are not shared or sold.

Website analytics

If analytics is switched on for this website, aggregate information about page visits (for example, which pages are viewed) is collected through Google Analytics. It is not part of, or linked to, a Community Connection response.
